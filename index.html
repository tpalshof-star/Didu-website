Why we made Didu

Curiosity is the best thing about people. It keeps the world from going flat, and it is the difference between passing through your life and paying attention to it.

We made Didu to feed that curiosity. One true thing every morning, for people who would always rather know.

Our ambition is simple. A year from now you know three hundred things you didn't know today, and you enjoyed finding out every one of them. Three of us make it, in Copenhagen and Kyiv.
