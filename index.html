Why we made this

The idea was Troels'. What if the first thing your phone gave you in the morning was something genuinely worth knowing? There are three of us building it now.

Troels

Copenhagen

Anna

Copenhagen

Anastasiia

Kyiv

Troels, in Copenhagen, has spent his career building companies and writing books. He's published several, including a bestselling thriller, and now advises companies on how to grow and adapt. Didu started about a year ago, in the garden over a glass of wine, with a question about what a phone could be for.

Anna, also in Copenhagen, spent close to twenty years in advertising before leaving to train as a nurse. She researches and writes the drops, curates what makes it in, and creates the images. She also gave Didu its name, from the question at the top of every drop: did you know? She and Troels have been together for ten years, and working on things together for almost as long.

Anastasiia, in Kyiv, is in her early twenties, and much of her adult life so far has been shaped by war. She designs the app itself, how it moves and how it feels to use, and cares more than most people would about building something calm.

Every drop is backed by two credible sources, checked before it goes out. Nothing goes out that we wouldn't want to read ourselves, and the images take hours each, because a picture that makes you look twice is doing as much work as the words.

We're early, so the people who join now are shaping what this becomes. If a drop makes you want to write back, we read every email at support@didu.app. And if there's something you think is worth knowing, we'd like to hear it.
