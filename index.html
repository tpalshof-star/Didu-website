We built the thing we were looking for.

For years I looked for something to read in the morning that could give me a little inspiration, perspective and reflection. It had to be true, and it had to be a pleasure to sit with. All of that at once turns out to be rare. In the end it was easier to build it than to keep looking.

Getting it this simple took a long time. A small thing that sends you into the day knowing something that puts the world in perspective, and then gets out of the way.

The part we did not expect: people repeat them. A drop read at seven turns up at lunch with a colleague, and again at dinner.
