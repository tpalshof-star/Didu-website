Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Didu.

Didu delivers one surprising, science-based fact to your phone every morning. Each "drop" takes under 2 minutes to read and covers topics from psychology to cosmology. No feed, no scroll. Just one calm idea to start your day.
Didu is free to try. After the trial, it's one price for the year. The exact amount is shown in your local currency in the App Store before you confirm. No ads, no hidden fees. You can cancel anytime through your App Store settings.
Every day of the week has its own theme: Sunday is Universe & Philosophy, Monday is Psychology & Behaviour, Tuesday is Science & Technology, Wednesday is History & Culture, Thursday is Economy & Society, Friday is Nature & Biology, and Saturday is Art & Creativity.
Every drop is researched, written and checked by hand. We look for findings that other researchers have already validated, rather than anything preliminary, and each drop carries two credible sources so you can go and read further yourself.
Didu is currently available on iOS through the App Store. Android is on our roadmap. Stay tuned.
One new drop arrives each day. Save the ones worth keeping and they stay in Saved for good. There's no back catalogue to scroll through: Didu is one a day, not a library to catch up on.
Once you have read and saved enough drops, short quizzes start arriving. Each one brings back a few of the mornings you have already had, so what you read stays something you know. They are not a test, and they take a minute.
