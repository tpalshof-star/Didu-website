What Didu is

Every day, Didu sends one carefully crafted insight to its readers. Each drop is backed by two credible academic sources, paired with a hand-picked image, and written to surprise and stay with you. We cover everything from neuroscience to ancient history, economics to ecology.

This is not a content mill. Every drop takes hours to research, write, and refine. We care deeply about accuracy, beauty, and the feeling of discovering something genuinely new.

Who we're looking for

We're always open to hearing from people who know something deeply and can explain it with clarity and wonder.

Researchers and academics

You've spent years studying something fascinating. We can help you share it with people who will actually appreciate it.

Science writers and journalists

You know how to take complex ideas and make them clear, compelling, and true. That's exactly what a Didu drop needs.

Curious experts of any kind

Maybe you're a historian, a marine biologist, a philosopher, or a data scientist. If you've ever made someone say "I had no idea," we want to talk.

What a good pitch looks like

You don't need a finished article. Just tell us: what's the surprising insight, why it matters, and what sources back it up. A few sentences is enough. If it sparks something, we'll work with you to shape it into a drop.
